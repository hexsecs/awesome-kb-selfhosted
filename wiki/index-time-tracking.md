# Time Tracking

- [ActivityWatch](activitywatch.md) — Automatically track how you spend time on your devices. (Source Code) MPL-2.0 Python
- [Beaver Habit Tracker](beaver-habit-tracker.md) — Habit tracking app to save your precious moments in your fleeting life. (Demo) BSD-3-Clause Docker
- [Ever Gauzy](ever-gauzy.md) — Open business management platform for collaborative, on-demand and sharing economies (ERP/CRM/HRM/ATS/PM). (Demo, Source Code) AGPL-3.0 Docker/Nodejs
- [Kimai](kimai.md) — Track work time and print out a summary of your activities on demand. (Demo, Source Code) AGPL-3.0 PHP
- [solidtime](solidtime.md) — Modern time tracking application for freelancers and agencies. (Source Code) AGPL-3.0 Docker
- [TimeTagger](timetagger.md) — Time-tracker based on an interactive timeline and powerful reporting. (Demo, Source Code) GPL-3.0 Python
- [TimeTracker](timetracker.md) — Track time across projects and clients, with timers, kanban tasks, CRM, expense tracking, multi-currency invoicing (PDF, Peppol/ZugFerd e-invoicing)…
- [Traggo](traggo.md) — Tag-based time tracking tool. In Traggo there are no tasks, only tagged time spans. (Source Code) GPL-3.0 Docker/Go
- [Wakapi](wakapi.md) — Tracking tool for coding statistics, compatible with WakaTime. (Source Code) GPL-3.0 Go/Docker
- [Ziit](ziit.md) — Swiss army knife of code time tracking (alternative to WakaTime). (Source Code) AGPL-3.0 Docker
