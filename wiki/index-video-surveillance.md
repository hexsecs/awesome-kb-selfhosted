- [SentryShot](sentryshot.md) — Video surveillance management system. GPL-2.0 Docker/Rust
- [Strix](strix.md) — Auto-discovers working stream URLs for IP cameras and generates ready-to-use Frigate and go2rtc configs. MIT Go/Docker
