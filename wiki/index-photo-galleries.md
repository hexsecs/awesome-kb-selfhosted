# Photo Galleries

- [Chevereto](chevereto.md) — Ultimate image sharing software. Create your very own personal image hosting website in just minutes. (Source Code) AGPL-3.0 PHP/Docker
- [ChronoFrame](chronoframe.md) — Personal gallery application with online photo management, supporting Live/Motion Photos, and explore map. (Demo, Source Code) MIT Nodejs/Docker
- [Damselfly](damselfly.md) — Fast server-based photo management system for large collections of images. Includes face detection, face & object recognition, powerful search, and EXIF…
- [Ente](ente.md) — End-to-end encrypted photo-sharing platform (alternative to Google Photos, Apple Photos). (Source Code) AGPL-3.0 Docker/Nodejs/Go
- [HomeGallery](homegallery.md) — Browse personal photos and videos featuring tagging, mobile-friendly, and AI powered image discovery. (Demo, Source Code) MIT Nodejs/Docker
- [Immich Kiosk](immich-kiosk.md) — Lightweight slideshow for running on kiosk devices and browsers that uses Immich as a data source. (Clients) GPL-3.0 Docker/Go
- [Immich](immich.md) — Photo and video backup solution directly from your mobile phone (alternative to Google Photos). (Demo, Source Code) AGPL-3.0 Docker
- [LibrePhotos](librephotos.md) — Photo management service with a slight focus on cool graphs (alternative to Google Photos). (Clients) MIT Python/Docker
- [Lychee](lychee.md) — Grid and album based photo-management-system. (Source Code) MIT PHP/Docker
- [Mediagoblin](mediagoblin.md) — Media publishing platform that anyone can run (alternative to Flickr, YouTube, SoundCloud). (Source Code) AGPL-3.0 Python
- [Memtly](memtly.md) — Event photo sharing platform and gallery with slideshow that allows guests to view and share memories via a QR code. (Demo, Source Code) GPL-3.0 C#/Docker
- [Nextcloud Memories](nextcloud-memories.md) — Fast, modern and advanced photo management suite. Runs as a Nextcloud app. (Demo, Source Code) AGPL-3.0 PHP
- [Photofield](photofield.md) — Experimental fast photo viewer. MIT Docker/Go
- [PhotoPrism](photoprism.md) — Personal photo management powered by Go and Google TensorFlow. Browse, organize, and share your personal photo collection, using the latest technologies to…
- [Photoview](photoview.md) — Simple and user-friendly photo gallery for personal servers. It is made for photographers and aims to provide an easy and fast way to navigate directories…
- [PiGallery 2](pigallery-2.md) — Directory-first photo gallery website, with a rich UI, optimised for running on low resource servers. (Source Code) MIT Docker/Nodejs
- [Piwigo](piwigo.md) — Photo gallery software for the web, built by an active community of users and developers. (Source Code) GPL-2.0 PHP
- [SPIS](spis.md) — Simple, lightweight and fast media server with decent mobile support. GPL-3.0 Docker/Rust
- [This week in past](this-week-in-past.md) — Aggregates images taken this week, from previous years and presents them on a web page with a simple slideshow. MIT Docker/Rust
- [Thumbor](thumbor.md) — Smart imaging service and enables on-demand cropping, resizing, applying filters and optimizing images. (Source Code) MIT Python/Docker
- [Zenphoto](zenphoto.md) — Gallery and CMS project. (Source Code) GPL-2.0 PHP
